mermaid2pptx

Turn a Mermaid flowchart, sequence diagram or Gantt chart into native, editable PowerPoint shapes. Runs entirely in your browser: nothing to install, and your diagram never leaves your computer.

Mermaid (type, paste, or drop a .mmd / .md file)

Preview (Mermaid's own rendering)

Open the downloaded .pptx, click the diagram (one group), copy it and paste it into your slide. With the Theme colours, choose your company template to see its colours in the download too. The first conversion loads Python in the page (about 15 MB, cached afterwards). Source, command-line version and documentation. MIT licence, provided as is, without warranty.
